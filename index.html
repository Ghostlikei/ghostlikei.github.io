I am a second year M.S. in Data Science (thesis track) at UC San Diego.

I’m working as a research assistant in Professor Biwei Huang’s lab, where I’m collaborating with and advised by Fan Feng (postdoc@UCSD/CMU) and Caleb Chuck (postdoc@Princeton). Previously, I received my bachelor’s degree in Data Science from East China Normal University, Shanghai.

Research interests

  • Unsupervised Reinforcement Learning
    • How can agents discover diverse, controllable skills through exploration without task-specific rewards?
    • How can we transfer skills above to downstream tasks?
  • Robot Learning
    • How can we learn behavior models/representations that prioritize control over visual reconstruction?
    • How can robots assess their own capabilities and continually evolve?
    • How can robots transfer skills to control unseen objects with little or no task-specific data?
